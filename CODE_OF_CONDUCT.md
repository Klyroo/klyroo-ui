# Code of Conduct

klyroo-ui is part of the Klyroo open-source ecosystem and adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text of the Covenant applies; this page summarizes it and says how to report a problem.

## Our pledge

We make participation in Klyroo a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences, and give and accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users, and take responsibility for your mistakes.
- Keep discussions technical and on topic in issues, pull requests and reviews.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, or publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in every Klyroo space (repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, chat) and when an
individual officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the Klyroo maintainers, using the contact listed on the
[Klyroo GitHub organization](https://github.com/klyroo). Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.
Maintainers review every report promptly and fairly, respect the privacy of the reporter, and apply the
[enforcement guidelines of the Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban or permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behavior.
