**Added**

- `/start` and `/fail` signals: a run now has a duration, and a failure opens an incident at once.
- The body of a `POST` ping is stored with it, up to 10 KB.

**Changed**

- The history of a monitor shows the duration of each run next to its time.

**Fixed**

- A ping that arrived during the maintenance of a paused monitor could resume it.
