---

**Added**

- Public status pages, with the last 90 days of each monitor and email subscriptions for incidents.
- The `tideline` command: `tideline run` wraps a job and sends the start, success and failure signals for you.
- A monitor list that shows a number only when it needs your attention.

**Changed**

- The ping address of a monitor now answers from the nearest region.
- The old dashboard is available in **Settings** until the end of the year.

**Fixed**

- A cron schedule written in a time zone with a half-hour offset no longer ran 30 minutes early.
