An account can have several people. What each of them can do depends on their role.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Viewer | See monitors, incidents and history. |
| Member | Everything a viewer can, and create, edit and pause monitors. |
| Admin | Everything a member can, and manage channels, people and billing. |

The Hobby plan has one user and no roles. The Team plan has up to 10 users, who are all members or admins. Roles, with viewers, come with the Scale plan, which has up to 50.

## Invite someone

1. Open **Settings**, then **People**.
2. Choose **Invite**, enter an email address and pick a role.
3. The invitation is valid for seven days. Until it is accepted, the person is listed as pending and does not count against your plan.

## The audit log

The audit log lists who did what and when: a monitor created, a schedule changed, a channel removed, a person invited. It is kept for the length of your history. Each line has the person, the action, the monitor or setting and the time, and the list can be filtered by person.

::: note Plans
The audit log is part of the Scale plan.
:::
