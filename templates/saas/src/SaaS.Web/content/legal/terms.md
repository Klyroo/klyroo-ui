## Using the service

You may use Tideline to monitor jobs that you own or are allowed to monitor. You are responsible for the account you create, for keeping its credentials safe and for what is done with it.

## Your data

The pings, the output you send with them and the settings of your monitors are yours. Tideline keeps them to run the service and for the length of the history of your plan, and deletes them when you delete your workspace.

## Availability

We aim for the service to be available at all times, but we do not promise it. A monitor is a way to notice a problem, not a replacement for a backup or for a person who is on call.

## Changes

We may change these terms. When we do, we write to the address of your account before the change applies.

## Contact
