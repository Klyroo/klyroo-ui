## What we collect

- **Account:** your name, your email address and a password hash, to let you sign in.
- **Monitors:** the names and schedules you set, and every ping with its time, duration and exit code.
- **Output you send:** the text in the body of a ping, up to 10 KB. Do not send secrets in it.

## What we do not collect

The site you are reading sets no tracking cookie and loads no script or font from another site. The only cookies are the ones that the pages need: your choice of language and the anti-forgery cookie of the forms.

## How long we keep it

The history of a monitor is kept for the length of your plan, from 7 days to a year. The account is kept until you delete it, and its data is removed within 30 days.

## Your choices

You can export or delete your data from **Settings**. For anything else, read the [terms of service](terms.md) or write to the address in the footer.
